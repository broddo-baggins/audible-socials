# Design direction, November 2026 (polish pass)

**Read:** a consumer concept demo for hiring managers and design-minded visitors, calm and bookish, with a restrained motion language. Dials: variance 6, motion 4, density 4. Mode: redesign, preserve. The "Marginalia" identity (ink on paper, one highlighter, a per-book waveform) from `DESIGN.md` stays; this pass finishes it.

## What the audit found (1440, 1280, 375, light and dark)

| Area | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | The app ignored `prefers-color-scheme`. Dark mode rendered the light paper. |
| Honesty | Visible copy (Home strip, footer, About, Terms, meta tags) called the project an "educational mock". No link back to the portfolio. |
| Home | The Now playing slab stretched to the height of the friends column and left a dead band under its buttons. |
| Mobile | The menu sheet had no scroll lock and no focus containment. The sticky nav and the assistant button crowd the bottom edge. |
| Hygiene | No automated check that every route loads without console errors or sideways scroll. |

## Direction

1. **Two themes, one system.** Semantic tokens gain a dark set. Paper goes near black, the player slab lifts to the lightest dark, the highlighter is unchanged and always carries dark ink. Washes become dim olive. All text pairs stay at 4.5:1 or better (ink 16:1, ink-2 10:1, ink-3 6.9:1).
2. **One accent, still.** `--hl` is a fill under ink, never text on paper. No new hues.
3. **Type scale unchanged.** Literata for titles and book names, Schibsted Grotesk for the interface, roles in `.t-*`.
4. **Honest framing.** A single pill, "Concept demo with sample data", links to https://amityogev.com/?cmd=projects. It sits in the Home strip, the footer and the mobile menu. No visible copy says mock, assignment, take-home or interview.
5. **Calm composition.** Slabs size to their content and anchor controls to the bottom edge instead of floating in empty space.
6. **Motion** is feedback only (press, reveal, progress) and collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
7. **Safety net.** A Playwright smoke loads every route at 1280 and 375 in both themes: no console errors, no horizontal scroll.

## Out of scope

Routes, IA, nav labels, data and the AI features are unchanged.
